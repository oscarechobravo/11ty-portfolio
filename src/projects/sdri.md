I’m starting on a new project led by long term collaborator Kelly Widdicks! Digital Research Infrastructure is a new topic for me. I have some passing knowledge about High Performance Clusters and what digital research looks like in Higher Education. From a little digging it seems that DRI is the term that policy makers use to describe the ICT/digital technology used in the context of scientific research. 

There’s a really good team of people who are interested in transdisciplinary perspectives on sustainability relating to DRI, so it’s not going to be a project that focuses on optimisation and efficiency, we might actually get to do some critical methods about DRI now and in sustainable futures.

### My Role

The project only started last week, so I’m not quite sure what my role will be entirely, some blend of user and design research, doing the user centred design and user research bit, but also spending time doing design work around what the future of DRI looks like.
