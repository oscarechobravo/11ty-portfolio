Meal Deal (2022)

Link

Meal Deal is now available on itch.io to print at home for free!

Meal Deal is a card game that offers worker-centric perspectives on gig economy courier work. The project is co-lead by myself and Ben Kirman and is grounded in the research projects Switch-Gig and Flip Gig.

Having started development in 2019 with Switch-Gig, this project has prioritised worker-led methods, producing a range of outputs that put forward the perspectives and experiences of gig economy couriers working in UK cities.

This project brings together outputs from:

  • Switch-Gig
  • FlipGig
  • Meal Deal

Tools and frameworks

Critical game design, speculative design, Research through Design
